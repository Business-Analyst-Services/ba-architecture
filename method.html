Phases and checkpoints

Each phase stopped at a confirmation checkpoint before anything downstream was built. Capability mapping takes two, because committing to level 3 before the level-1 and level-2 structure is agreed wastes the decomposition.

  1. Checkpoint 1A — 7 domains and 30 level-2 capabilities. Confirmed as drafted. The alternative considered and rejected was narrowing to consulting plus training, which would have dropped Platform & Technology.
  2. Checkpoint 1B — the 81 level-3 capabilities with a business object on each. Confirmed as drafted.
  3. Checkpoint 2 — 57 business objects consolidated to 22 concept objects, classified and related. Confirmed as drafted. Rejected alternatives: splitting Outcome & Insight into benefit and insight, promoting Lead to its own object, and collapsing further to about 15.
  4. Checkpoint 3 — the stage draft for both streams. Confirmed with both streams retained, rather than consulting only, a single merged stream, or a split of the final stage.

Calls that could reasonably go the other way

  • Lead folded into Opportunity. Treating unqualified interest as an early state of Opportunity suits a practice where enquiries are few and personally handled. A real marketing funnel needs Lead as its own record.
  • Practice Strategy folded into Service Offering. This makes strategy a property of the portfolio rather than a thing the practice manages. The cost is that strategic capabilities have nowhere obvious to attach.
  • Outcome & Insight as one object. It carries benefits realised, learner feedback, research findings and usage metrics. That breadth is what lets it close the loop in one relationship, and it is also the reason someone could argue it is two objects wearing one name.
  • Assessment inside Course, Certificate inside Enrolment. Assessment design is a resource; a marked assessment is evidence. Splitting them across two objects is a judgement, not a rule.
  • No third value stream. The most consequential omission. Six capabilities are stranded by it.

What was verified

Checks that were actually run, not an aspiration:

  • Referential integrity across the bundle. Every parent, concept, capability, value-object, role, outcome, metric and stage reference resolves to an element that exists, and every map key equals its own element ID.
  • Consolidation completeness. All 57 capability-map business objects resolve to exactly one of the 22 concept objects.
  • Coverage. 67 of 81 capabilities map to a stage; the 14 that do not are enumerated in the bundle metadata and match the computed set.
  • Workbooks. Each register opens with its four expected tabs and recalculates with zero formula errors under LibreOffice. Rollups reconcile: domain counts to 81, percentages to 100%, relationship counts to 35 outbound and 35 inbound.
  • Accessibility. Every figure has a text alternative and a table view, every interactive mark is keyboard-reachable and labelled, and the colour palette was validated rather than eyeballed — see below.

Colour, and why it is not the brand palette

The practice's brand hexes are navy #23446E and #003E52, teal #5ABABA and orange #FF8300. Used directly as the seven domain colours they fail a categorical palette check: the two navies fall outside the usable lightness band, and three of the four sit below the chroma floor, which means they read as grey once they are small.

So the data colours keep the brand hue angles and re-step the lightness and chroma into the passing band, with four further hues spaced around the wheel. The resulting order was validated for colour-blind separation and surface contrast in both light and dark modes, and the dark steps were chosen for the dark surface rather than flipped from the light ones.

Raw brand colours still do the chrome — wordmark, buttons, the accent on the current nav item. Every chart also labels its marks directly and ships a table view, so no reading of any figure depends on telling two colours apart.

Honest limitations

  • The content is self-reported in a single session. Nobody else was interviewed, and no client or learner was asked whether the stages match their experience.
  • Metrics are proposed, never baselined. No value in the model has been measured.
  • Capability maturity and ownership are not modelled at all. The map says what the practice can do, not how well or who is accountable.
  • The third value stream is missing, and six capabilities are stranded as a result.
  • The level-3 count, 81, is on the large side for a practice this size. It is honest about the breadth of work but would be hard to govern as a live artefact without pruning.

Rebuilding the site

The pages are generated from the model data, so the data is the source of truth and the HTML is disposable:

python3 build.py

Edit data/capabilities.json, data/concepts.json or data/valuestreams.json and re-run. No dependencies beyond the Python standard library.
