The business architecture of a business analysis practice

Business Analyst Services teaches business architecture. This is the practice modelled with its own method — a capability map, a concept model and two value streams, built in one session and published whole, including the parts that are still rough.

What the model argues

Read in sequence the three artefacts make one claim, and it is a claim about the business rather than about the diagrams.

Both value streams terminate in the same place. Consulting delivery ends at Realise & Reinvest; a training cohort ends at Sustain & Improve. Each exits into Outcome & Insight, and that object feeds back into IP Asset and Course — the two resources the next engagement and the next cohort are built from.

That loop is the difference between a consultancy that sells hours and one that compounds. An engagement that produces only deliverables leaves nothing behind; an engagement that also produces a playbook makes the next one cheaper to deliver and easier to sell. The model makes the loop explicit so it can be measured — which is why reusable assets harvested per engagement sits in the metric set alongside days sales outstanding.

Where it is incomplete

14 of 81 capabilities sit outside both value streams. Most are genuinely enabling — vendor selection and subscription administration do not advance a value item for anyone.

But two clusters are not: manuscript authoring, editorial review and publishing, and product backlog management, software development and automated testing. A book shipped and the platform is a real product, so a third stream — IP to Published Asset — would likely absorb all six. It is not modelled here. See the full list.

Using it

  • As a reference. The shape — 7 domains, Party/Resource/Record, stages defined by entry and exit state — transfers to any professional services practice. The content does not.
  • As data. Every artefact is downloadable as a four-tab register, and the whole model as a single JSON bundle for import into VCC or the PlausibleBA canvas. Downloads.
  • As a check on the method. The method page records what was elicited, what was decided at each checkpoint, and which calls are arguable.
