Phase 3

Value streams

How value actually reaches someone. The practice monetises through two distinct paths, so both are modelled. Stages are defined by the state of the value item when it arrives and when it leaves — not by activity, not by who does the work.

  • Practice Strategy & Governance
  • Client Engagement & Advisory
  • Knowledge & IP Development
  • Learning & Training
  • Market Development
  • Platform & Technology
  • Business Operations

Capability chips are coloured by domain and always carry their name, so the colour is a grouping cue rather than the information itself.

Capabilities outside both streams

67 of 81 capabilities are called on by a stage. The remaining 14 are not, and that is worth being explicit about rather than hiding.

  • Manuscript Authoring KID-2.1
  • Editorial Review KID-2.2
  • Publishing & Distribution KID-2.3
  • Brand Management MKT-1.1
  • Campaign Performance Analysis MKT-2.3
  • Partner Relationship Management MKT-4.1
  • Vendor Selection OPS-3.1
  • Subscription Administration OPS-3.2
  • Product Backlog Management PLT-1.1
  • Software Development PLT-1.2
  • Automated Testing PLT-1.3
  • Data Protection & Privacy PLT-4.2
  • Market Positioning Definition PSG-1.1
  • Service Portfolio Planning PSG-1.2

Most of these are genuinely enabling. Vendor Selection and Subscription Administration support delivery without advancing anyone's value item. Market Positioning Definition and Service Portfolio Planning are strategic — they shape what the streams carry rather than running inside one.

Two clusters are a real gap. Manuscript Authoring, Editorial Review and Publishing & Distribution describe a book that has shipped. Product Backlog Management, Software Development and Automated Testing describe a platform that exists. Both produce something for someone outside the practice, which is the test of a value stream. A third stream — IP to Published Asset, running from research through to a published, licensed asset — would absorb all six. It is not modelled here, and the model is weaker for it.
