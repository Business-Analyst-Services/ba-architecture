How to read it

  • A capability is an ability, not a process or a team. “Requirements Elicitation” is something the practice can do; it says nothing about who does it, in what order, or how often.
  • Every level-3 capability names the object it acts on. That column is what seeds the concept model — 57 business objects surfaced here, consolidated to 22 there.
  • The map is deliberately whole-practice. Consulting delivery, training, IP and publishing, and the platform all appear. A services-only map would be tidier and would hide where the leverage is.
