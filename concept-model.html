Phase 2

Concept model

What the practice manages: the things it holds facts about, and how they relate. The 81 capabilities surfaced 57 business objects; those consolidate to 22 concept objects with 35 relationships. Every one of the 57 is accounted for — none were dropped.

The model

  • Party — who acts
  • Resource — what is used
  • Record — what is evidenced

Objects are grouped in bands by type and labelled with their type name, so colour is never the only cue. Hovering or focusing an object dims everything it does not connect to. Each object's full definition and every relationship are listed in the tables below.

The spine

Six of the 35 relationships carry the consulting business: Client is the source of an Opportunity, which converts to a Contract, which authorises an Engagement, which produces a Deliverable, which is evaluated by Outcome & Insight.

Training runs in parallel: Learner registers via an Enrolment held against a Course, which is sourced from an IP Asset, which embodies the Methodology.

The two meet at Outcome & Insight, which improves both IP Asset and Course. That is the only cycle in the model, and it is intentional.

One object has no origin in the capability map. Client was introduced during consolidation as the party behind Opportunity and Contract. No capability names it as the thing it acts on, which is itself worth noticing: the practice has no capability whose object is the client relationship. The other 21 concept objects each consolidate at least one of the 57 business objects the capabilities surfaced.

Two calls worth arguing with. Lead was folded into Opportunity as an early state rather than kept as its own object — defensible for a practice of this size, wrong for one with a real marketing funnel. Practice Strategy was folded into Service Offering rather than becoming a governance object, which makes strategy invisible in the model.
